Paths to a Payoff

A company takes on n projects. Each one succeeds or fails with probability ½, independently of the others. A success pays +$2M; a failure costs −$1M. Every sequence of outcomes is a path from the top of the lattice to a total at the bottom, and every path is equally likely. So the probability of a total is the number of paths reaching it, divided by 2n.
